### A1-03  (A1)  Verification-aware payment scheduler using virtual output queues and iterative matching to remove urgency-driven verification bypass
Problem: Social-engineering scams (such as AI-generated executive messages) exploit cut-off pressure. Instructions awaiting out-of-band verification block FIFO payment queues, and operators under time pressure release them unverified.
Mechanism: The payment hub keeps a virtual queue per (verification resource, risk class) pair instead of one FIFO. Each scheduling slot, an iterative request-grant-accept matcher with rotating round-robin pointers (as in crossbar switch schedulers) allocates scarce verification resources to pending instructions: call-back agents, biometric re-authentication, dual-control approvers. An instruction whose verification cannot complete before its cut-off is rescheduled deterministically to the next settlement cycle and the customer is told why.
