## Reading of the landscape (what it means for our ideas)
- **The incumbents' AI filings are about internal productivity** (LLM chunking, document generation, planning assistants, code generation, audit automation). Very few are about **agentic payment risk at network scale**. This supports the whitespace behind #5 (agent-herding detection) and the agent-control ideas A1-07 and A2b-09.
- **Authentication filings** (Capital One, Visa, Wells, Amex) concentrate on credentials, keys and biometrics. **None cover evidentiary co-presence** (#7) or **per-channel OTP attribution** (#6).
- **Nothing in this sample addresses credit-reporting dispute provenance** (#2, #4) or **cross-bank pre-emptive fund interception** (#1). Those whitespaces line up with the scanner's demand signals.
- **Coinbase dominates blockchain filings** (28 of 39). Stablecoin reserve and redemption ideas (A2b-03, A1-23) would enter a space where a crypto-native firm is filing heavily. Look at Coinbase's claims before filing.
- **Indian IT vendors (TCS, Infosys) file heavily on ML and LLM tooling**. These are potential licensees or partners for the bank-side ideas, especially #1 through NPCI-connected platforms (Finacle, BaNCS).
