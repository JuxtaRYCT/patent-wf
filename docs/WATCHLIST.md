# Watchlist: strong ideas that are *not* filing candidates as-is

These ideas are creative and technically credible but failed the novelty bar, have weak eligibility, or need partners. Each has a recommended route: narrow the claim, publish defensively (to block competitors from patenting it), or pursue as research.

| ID | Idea | Why it is not a filing candidate now | Recommended route |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1-01 | Grid-hum (ENF) liveness for voice-confirmed payment instructions | DeFakePro (2022) and "Deterring deepfake attacks with ENF fingerprints" do real-time ENF deepfake detection. IBM US10957355B2 covers emitted-signal authentication. | Dependent claim inside a broader voice-instruction-verification filing; otherwise defensive publication |
| A1-06 | Living QR: flicker-coded payment codes read by event sensors | ScreenID (MobiCom'20), Revelio (2025), US12114003, Denso Wave US12367364B2 | Narrow claim: payee-key-authenticated rolling nonce plus relay-latency bound. Worth a targeted search for NPCI/UPI QR |
